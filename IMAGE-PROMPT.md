## 1. Who the images are for

**AOD (Artists On Demand)** is a booking platform from **Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India**. People book verified event artists through it, as easily as booking a cab:

- **Customers:** families planning weddings, sangeets, receptions, birthdays and parties.
- **Businesses:** companies planning conferences, product launches, expos and brand shoots.

AOD's real work includes official coverage of the **GCCI GATE Expo 2026** in Gandhinagar.

The website is being redesigned in a very clean, minimal, **Apple / Steve Jobs-like** style:

- White page, black text, lots of empty space.
- One accent colour: **AOD orange (#FF6201)**, used only for small details.
- No decoration and no clutter.

The images go in a **photo grid right under the homepage heading**, which reads "Book A Photographer", "Book A Cinematographer" and so on. They are the first thing a visitor sees, so they must look like **real, premium event photography of real Indian artists at work**.

## 4. The look (all 9 must look like one set)

**Reference:** the attached grid photo. Take its **style**, not its subjects:
- Real people caught in the middle of their work, in real places.
- Natural, believable light.
- Warm and honest, not glossy or fake.

**Especially the "Photography & Cinema" tile in the reference** (a man in a black T-shirt behind a cinema camera on a tripod, in a bright studio with a softbox). That is the target feel for every image:
- **Subject:** a focused professional, mid-task, not posing for the camera.
- **Setting:** a clean, bright place that is easy to read.
- **Look:** soft daylight or soft studio light, realistic skin, a slightly warm and natural colour grade.
- **Framing:** a medium shot that shows the person and the tool of their craft.

For every image:
- **Realism:** photorealistic, like a photo taken on a professional camera (full-frame, 35mm or 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, gentle background blur). Real textures, real skin, real fabric; no plastic or over-smoothed skin.
- **People:** Indian people (mostly Gujarati and North Indian looks) in their 20s–40s, a mix of men and women across the set. They look like **skilled professionals**: confident, focused, well-dressed for their work.
- **Light:** natural daylight, warm golden-hour light, or the real stage and event lights of the venue. Bright and clean overall; avoid dark, moody or heavily tinted images. The stage shots (DJ, singer, anchor, comedian) can have stage lights, but keep them **warm and natural**.
- **Colour:** natural and slightly warm, the same grade across all 9. Real Indian event colours (marigold, red and gold wedding wear, festive lights) are welcome. The set should feel bright and premium, not saturated or neon.
- **Composition:** one main subject, filling a good part of the frame. Simple background. Room around the subject so a square crop works.
- **Mood:** joyful, professional, trustworthy. Families and businesses should look at it and think "these are the people I want at my event".

## 5. Rules (every image)

1. **Square, 1:1.** At least **1024 × 1024**; **2048 × 2048** for the two large tiles (2 and 6).
2. **No text of any kind:** no captions, labels, titles, numbers, logos, watermarks, signs or banners with readable words, and no brand names on clothes or equipment.
3. **No graphic blocks:** no solid colour squares, no black background with white text, no purple or red-orange panels, no borders or frames. **Only the photo.**
4. **Photographs only:** no illustration, 3D render, cartoon, painting or collage look.
5. **One image = one scene.** No split screens, grids or multiple panels inside one image.
6. **Hands and faces must be correct:** five fingers, natural hands on the equipment, natural eyes and teeth. Redo an image if they look wrong.
7. **Real, current equipment:** mirrorless cameras, cinema cameras, gimbals, small drones, wireless microphones, DJ controllers, and so on. Equipment can't show brand names.
8. **No famous people** and nobody who looks like a specific real celebrity or politician.
9. **Respectful and decent:** modest, event-appropriate clothing, nothing revealing.
10. **India-specific settings:** no Western-only details such as snow, American houses or Western wedding chapels.
